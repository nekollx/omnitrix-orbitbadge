OMNITRIX OS

1-Click Browser Firmware Installer for the OrbitBadge (ESP32-S3).
No coding, drivers, or Arduino IDE required.

  1. Open this page in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Opera on a PC or Mac.
  2. Turn your OrbitBadge's Power Slide Switch ON and connect it via a data-capable USB-C cable.
  3. Click Install Omnitrix below, select the USB JTAG / Serial Debug Unit port, and click Install.
Your browser does not support Web Serial. Please open this page in Chrome or Edge on desktop. Web Serial requires HTTPS or localhost.
